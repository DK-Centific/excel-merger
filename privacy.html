Privacy Policy

What this tool does with your data.

Merge is an internal Centific tool for our data-delivery team. It combines agency participant spreadsheets and fills in Dropbox links for review before client delivery. This page explains what data it touches and where it goes.

Last updated 2026-08-11

Who this is for

This is an internal tool used by Centific staff who work on data-delivery projects. It is not a public consumer product, and it is not intended for use outside Centific.

Where your data goes

Merge runs entirely in your web browser. There is no backend server — nothing you open or process is ever uploaded to us or to any third party we control. The two places data moves are:

  • Your device's memory, while a spreadsheet is open in the browser tab.
  • Dropbox's own API, when you connect your Dropbox account and the tool reads folders, downloads spreadsheets, or resolves/creates shared links on your behalf.

Closing the tab clears everything that was loaded. Nothing is written to a database anywhere.

What it reads from Dropbox

Using your own Dropbox sign-in, and only for folders your account can already access:

  • Folder and file listings, to find the agency spreadsheets and media files.
  • The content of .xlsx spreadsheets only — video, photo and PDF files are never downloaded, only linked.
  • Existing shared links, so a second run reuses them instead of creating duplicates.

It can also create a Dropbox shared link, but only for a file you've reviewed and only after you click a confirmation button showing exactly how many links will be created.

What kind of data is in the spreadsheets

The spreadsheets processed by this tool can contain participant names, contact emails, guardian names and contact details, birthdates, and links to media files. This is why the tool is browser-only with no server component — that data should not pass through infrastructure beyond what the delivery team already controls in Dropbox.

Sign-in

Signing in to Dropbox is per person — each user connects their own account, and the tool only ever sees what that account can already open. Centific does not receive or store your Dropbox password; sign-in goes directly through Dropbox's own login page.

Cookies and tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking scripts. The site stores a small amount of configuration (like which folder to open by default) in your browser's local storage, and your Dropbox session token in your browser's session storage so you don't have to sign in every time. Both stay on your device.

Questions

This tool is maintained internally at Centific. If you have questions about this policy or how the tool handles data, contact the team that shared this link with you.
